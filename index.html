Frequently asked questions

Where are my saved games?
Hexarch saves automatically after every turn. Tap Continue on the title screen, or Load to pick any saved game.

How do I unlock the Seafarers, Nomads and Machina?
By playing: win one match for the Seafarers, three for the Nomads, and win a match on Hard or Brutal to unlock the Machina boss. Your progress is shown under Records.

Rival turns take too long.
Open Settings → Rival turn speed and choose Fast or Instant. You can also tap Skip during rival turns.

Can I play with friends?
Yes — switch a rival to Human for pass-and-play on one device, or use Share Match in the game menu to send the match to a friend, who imports it from the title screen's Load sheet.

Is there anything to buy?
No. Hexarch has no ads and no in-app purchases.
