HEXARCH

Build · Trade · Conquer

Hexarch is a turn-based strategy game for iPhone and iPad. Lift the clouds, found your capital and outplay rival civilizations on a hex map with a living economy. Made in Switzerland by SwissGrat.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my saved games?
Hexarch saves automatically after every turn. Tap Continue on the title screen, or Load to pick any saved game.

How do I unlock the Seafarers, Nomads and Machina?
By playing: win one match for the Seafarers, three for the Nomads, and win a match on Hard or Brutal to unlock the Machina boss. Your progress is shown under Records. If you don't want to wait, the optional Unlock Everything purchase in the Store unlocks them right away, together with the Gilded look.

Is there anything to buy?
Hexarch is free to play, with no ads and no energy or timers. There is one optional one-time purchase, Unlock Everything, and three optional tips to support the developer. Purchases give no gameplay advantage — everything they unlock can also be earned by playing.

I bought something on another device / after reinstalling.
Open Settings → Restore Purchases (also in the Store). Purchases are linked to your Apple Account and work with Family Sharing. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I play with friends?
Yes. Tap Online Games on the title screen to play turn-based matches with friends through Game Center. You can also switch a rival to Human for pass-and-play on one device, or use Share Match in the game menu to send a match file to a friend, who imports it from the title screen's Load sheet.

Online Games says I need to sign in.
Online matches, leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center. Sign in under iOS Settings → Game Center. Everything else in Hexarch works without it.

Rival turns take too long.
Open Settings → Rival turn speed and choose Fast or Instant. You can also tap Skip during rival turns.

Which languages are supported?
Hexarch follows your device language and is available in 20 languages. You can change it for Hexarch only in iOS Settings → Apps → Hexarch → Language.
